SUPPLY AGREEMENT (EXTRACT)
Between the Target ("Seller") and Bramwell Distribution Ltd ("Buyer")
Contract reference SUP-2024-0112. Extract prepared for the data room.

Every restriction below is adverse to the Seller, and none of it is written in
the words a keyword search would look for. This document exists to show the
difference between matching a phrase and understanding a clause: the literal
matcher finds nothing here, and the embedding pass should still recognise what
these terms are.

1. DURATION
   1.1 This agreement runs to 31 December 2027 and thereafter continues for
       further periods of one year at a time, ending only if either party
       serves notice six months before an anniversary.

2. ENDING THE AGREEMENT
   2.1 The Buyer may bring this agreement to an end at any time, for any reason
       or for no reason, by giving one month's notice in writing. No payment
       falls due to the Seller as a result.
   2.2 If the persons who ultimately own or control the Seller cease to do so,
       or if a third party acquires the ability to direct the Seller's affairs,
       the Buyer may end this agreement with immediate effect.

3. TRANSFER
   3.1 The Seller may not pass the benefit or burden of this agreement to any
       other person, nor engage anyone else to perform it, unless the Buyer has
       first agreed in writing. The Buyer is under no obligation to agree.

4. MAKING GOOD LOSSES
   4.1 The Seller shall make good every loss, claim, cost and expense the Buyer
       suffers in connection with the goods, without limit as to amount and
       without regard to any cap stated elsewhere in this agreement.
   4.2 No financial ceiling applies to the Seller's obligations under clause 4.1
       in respect of confidentiality, data protection or third party rights.

5. DEALING WITH OTHERS
   5.1 During the term the Seller shall not supply goods of the same or similar
       description to any other buyer in the United Kingdom.
   5.2 For two years after this agreement ends, the Seller shall not carry on or
       be engaged in any business which competes with the Buyer within the same
       market.

6. FAILURE TO DELIVER
   6.1 For each week of late delivery the Seller shall pay the Buyer a fixed sum
       of 2% of the annual contract value, agreed in advance as a fair estimate
       of the harm caused, recoverable whether or not actual loss is proved.

7. DISPUTES
   7.1 The courts of Northern Ireland shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any
       dispute arising under this agreement, and the law applied shall be that
       of Northern Ireland.
