MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (EXTRACT)
Between the Target ("Supplier") and Northgate Retail Group plc ("Customer")
Contract reference MSA-2023-0041. Extract prepared for the data room.

This is a deliberately one-sided customer agreement, used to exercise contract
review on terms that are commercially adverse to the seller. It carries no
instructions to the reader and is not an injection fixture; for that, see
fixtures/deal_documents_tampered.

1. TERM AND RENEWAL
   1.1 The initial term is thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date.
   1.2 On expiry the agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve
       (12) month periods unless the Customer gives ninety (90) days notice. The
       Supplier has no corresponding right not to renew.

2. TERMINATION
   2.1 The Customer may terminate this agreement in whole or in part by
       termination for convenience on thirty (30) days written notice, without
       liability and without payment of any termination sum.
   2.2 The Supplier may terminate only for the Customer's material breach
       remaining unremedied after ninety (90) days.
   2.3 Change of control: the Customer may terminate immediately on any change
       of control of the Supplier, including any transfer of more than 25% of
       the Supplier's issued share capital, whether or not the Customer's
       interests are prejudiced by that change.

3. ASSIGNMENT
   3.1 The Supplier shall not assign, novate, subcontract or otherwise transfer
       this agreement or any part of it without prior written consent of the
       Customer, such consent to be granted or withheld at the Customer's
       absolute discretion.
   3.2 The Customer may assign freely to any member of its group.

4. INDEMNITY AND LIABILITY
   4.1 The Supplier shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Customer, its
       group companies, officers and agents against all losses, damages, costs
       and expenses of any nature arising out of or in connection with the
       services, and such indemnity shall be an uncapped indemnity not subject
       to any financial limit or aggregate cap.
   4.2 The Supplier accepts unlimited liability for breach of confidentiality,
       breach of data protection obligations, and any infringement of third
       party intellectual property rights.
   4.3 The Customer's aggregate liability to the Supplier under this agreement
       shall not exceed the charges paid in the three (3) months preceding the
       claim.

5. SERVICE CREDITS AND LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
   5.1 Failure to meet the availability target in any month entitles the
       Customer to liquidated damages of 5% of the annual charges per incident,
       which the parties agree is a genuine pre-estimate of loss.
   5.2 Service credits are not the Customer's exclusive remedy and are without
       prejudice to any other right, including the indemnity at clause 4.1.

6. EXCLUSIVITY AND RESTRAINT
   6.1 The Supplier grants the Customer exclusivity in the United Kingdom
       retail grocery sector for the term and for twelve (12) months following
       expiry.
   6.2 The Supplier accepts a non-compete covenant preventing it from providing
       equivalent services to any of the twenty (20) entities listed in
       Schedule 4 for the same period.

7. PRICING
   7.1 Charges are fixed for the initial term and thereafter increase by no more
       than CPI, save that the Customer may require an annual efficiency
       reduction of 3% to the then-current charges.

8. AUDIT
   8.1 The Customer may audit the Supplier's books, records and premises on
       five (5) business days notice, up to four times per year, at the
       Supplier's cost where any material discrepancy is identified.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
   9.1 This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales and the
       parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the English courts.
